    How to measure

    Pick one landmark on or beside the road as zero (a mailbox, a post). Measure along the road with a tape or wheel to 3–6 more landmarks spread across the view, negative toward the camera, positive away. Mark both pavement edges and the centre line with a few clicks each, spread along their length. Then outline the pavement area where cars should be measured.
